ka-strokes.json: Georgian (Mkhedruli) letter strokes traced from the centre line of
Noto Sans Georgian (SIL Open Font License 1.1, https://fonts.google.com/noto). Order and direction are approximate.

el-strokes.json: Greek capital and small letters drawn by hand for Kidsland, in the usual school stroke order.
